# 2018 Street Glide — mechanic build plan

Updated October 23, 2026 · Harley-Davidson FLHX · Read with the [decision register](DECISIONS.md) and [parts catalog](../README.md).

The job is to assemble the purchased 129-inch engine, induction, cooling, exhaust and primary-drive parts; fit the corrected Touring brake package; and integrate them with the equipment the owner is keeping. The Kraus front-end and control package is selected and quoted, with payment and order conversion still unverified. Purchased or delivered does not mean installed: confirm the physical inventory at intake and record what is already on the motorcycle.

## Instructions already settled

- **BAKER compensator: CLOSED.** The correct **730-M8 Touring** version is ordered. The packing slip and the owner's correction supersede the earlier Softail wording.
- **OG brakes: CLOSED.** The corrected kit is **Touring, hub mount, dual disc**, with **black calipers, gold spacers and black brackets**. The old Softail/Dyna label is superseded. The remaining work is installation planning for the chosen fork and wheel, not reopening the order correction.
- **PM Vision cam cover stays in scope; PM Vision derby cover is cancelled.** A derby service gasket in the BAKER rebuild kit is not another derby cover.
- **Keep the current Rekluse RadiusCX, upgraded basket and slave cylinder; Öhlins HD 778 shocks; Soundstream Reserve V2 with existing iDataLink Maestro; and CHIGEE AIO-6 LTE.** Both screens and the Hertz audio system stay.

## 2. Engine, induction, exhaust and cooling

Do the engine work as one measured assembly, with the actual heads, pistons, cam, rocker system and fasteners on the bench before final assembly.

- [ ] Inspect the used heads and document condition, casting identity, chamber measurements and the actual spring/retainer/valve combination.
- [ ] Resolve the spring combination before closing the heads. [Harley J06401](https://serviceinfo.harley-davidson.com/sip/service/document/1874975677612623235?locale=en_GB) gives **.540-inch maximum lift for the stock springs in its listed head kits**; the [S&S 550C](https://www.sscycle.com/products/550c-chain-drive-camshaft-kit-for-2017-up-m8-models/) has **.550-inch valve lift**. This does not identify the springs currently in the used heads. The Power Pack supplies a high-lift spring kit; verify its suitability and record installed height, available travel, retainer-to-guide/seal clearance and piston-to-valve clearance for the finished assembly.
- [ ] Record piston/deck/gasket/chamber measurements and the resulting compression ratio. Do not copy the Power Pack's advertised baseline ratio onto this mixed-head build.
- [ ] Reconcile the Guardian's roller rockers, shafts, rocker studs and **900-1006 head bolts**. Those head bolts cover the separately specified Power Pack head-bolt requirement. The purchased cylinder studs are a different part group, not duplicate rocker studs.
- [ ] Install and check the piston jets, cylinder studs, pump, cam plate, cam drive, tappets, cuffs and adjustable pushrods using the instructions for the actual supplied revisions. Record the critical measurements and the instruction revision used.
- [ ] Dry-fit the **HPi 62 mm throttle body/manifold, 6.3 injectors, high-flow fuel rail, 0.940 external breather and Feuling BA** together. Check support, sealing, hose path and air-cleaner clearance before final tightening.
- [ ] Confirm the HPi external-breather/Feuling interface specifically. [HPi's system](https://www.horsepowerinc.net/product-page/billet-pro-external-breather-kit) is described for HPi air cleaners and selected flat-backplate applications; [Feuling 5434](https://www.feulingparts.com/sku/5434/ba-air-cleaner-kit-black-finish-red-filter) listing HPI 62 mm compatibility does not by itself validate this additional breather stack. Resolve the mounting/sealing arrangement with the supplied instructions or HPi before assembly.
- [ ] Fit the [complete high-flow rail kit](https://www.horsepowerinc.net/product-page/high-flow-fuel-rail), including its tank connection. The kit changes the factory quick-connect arrangement. Verify the actual tank/outlet, fittings, hose routing and leak-free fuel operation; obtain the packed rail instructions where no public manual is available.
- [ ] Preserve the removed throttle-body core for the Fuel Moto return. The order used the **$249 return-core-later** option.
- [ ] Fit the delivered HPi **HPI-EXM8B-S full-length M8 2017–2023** exhaust with its supplied hardware. Record O2 sensor arrangement and clearance to the new oil system, bodywork, foot controls and luggage.
- [ ] Fit OilBud **SQ7212655** with the ordered K&P filter; verify hose paths, filter service access, sensor clearance and the relationship to existing cooler covers. Do not retain an older accessory cooler in series by assumption.
- [ ] Fit the PM Vision **cam** cover. Use the exact M8 Vision instructions or supplied packet: PM's generic downloadable Cam Cover ZIP was found to contain an older Scallop-cover instruction, not this cover's verified instructions.

The tuner needs the final measured engine specification, injector data and O2 arrangement before first running and calibration. Record which Power Vision device and VIN license are used, save the original and final files, and document break-in/service instructions from the applicable engine-component manufacturers.

## 3. Primary drive and retained Rekluse system

Open the primary once for the compensator, manual chain adjuster and rebuild seals while keeping the owner's clutch system.

- [ ] Identify the installed RadiusCX kit, basket and slave labels so the correct Rekluse setup sheet is used. The **product identity is owner-confirmed**; exact component part numbers and settings still need to be recorded.
- [ ] Inventory the PRK-FL-M8 contents and fit the applicable seals/gaskets as the assembly is opened. Do not treat its derby service kit as a replacement decorative cover.
- [ ] Install the corrected **730-M8** and check compensator/clutch chain alignment with the supplied shims as directed by the [BAKER manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1034/8027/files/Compensating_Sprocket_Instructions_PV5-121105.pdf?v=1618502420). The M8 kit does not use the **617-67 spring-pack spacer**.
- [ ] Set the Armored Attitude Adjuster against the chain's tightest position using its instructions. Record the specified follow-up adjustment/inspection interval.
- [ ] Check Rekluse free-play gain, engagement, release, hydraulic operation and hot operation to its exact kit instructions. Keep the six blue spare springs separate unless their identity and a deliberate setup change are documented.

Use each assembly's actual fastener specification and lubricant/locking-compound instruction. This plan supplies no substitute torque chart for mixed OEM, BAKER, S&S, Rekluse or titanium hardware.

## 4. Front end, brakes, bars and rear docking

First confirm whether the quoted Kraus shipment is available. Its selected configuration is **KR8 ST-IF-84-0-BB, 30 mm stem, standard length, performance valving, black**, plus **Open Road KT-ST-SG-232-B, 13.5-inch total height**. Keep that selection distinct from the brake kit already ordered.

- [ ] Set out the final wheel, hub-mount OG rotors, calipers, axle/spacers and fork parts together. Use the [KR8 assembly guide](https://krausmotorco.com/kr8-inverted-front-end-installation-guide/) to establish the mounting stack if the KR8 is fitted. The OG kit's conventional-fork adapters are not automatically added to a KR8 radial mounting stack.
- [ ] Check rotor centering, caliper/pad sweep, fastener engagement and wheel/ABS-sensor spacing with the actual parts. Preserve the selected black/gold/black finish and note any kit pieces that remain unused.
- [ ] Reconcile hardware quantities: the quote has **two UN-BR-T80-BB caliper hardware kits** but **one HD-T516-B5 rotor-bolt pack**. Kraus calls for two rotor packs for dual discs; identify the source of the second rotor's correct fasteners before assembly.
- [ ] Route the quoted **EL-2000 lower brake lines** using the [Kraus diagram](https://krausmotorco.com/content/pdfs/Brake-Line-Diagram.pdf). Confirm the upper line and clutch/control wiring independently for the final bar height; the lower-line kit does not establish upper-line length.
- [ ] Check all lines and harnesses at steering lock and through suspension travel. Complete the required ABS-capable bleed procedure if ABS is fitted, then check release, lever feel, leakage and wheel rotation.
- [ ] Establish rider reach and the key-switch/steering-lock arrangement before modifying the nacelle for the Open Road/T-Rex assembly. The stock switch/lock does not remain in its original position with that plate.
- [ ] Retain **HD 778** rear shocks and record spring code, preload, damping settings and sag. If fitting Pro-4 docking **ST-SC-41-B**, check its clamp dimensions and reservoir/hose position against those actual shocks. The current Pro-4 catalog names other Öhlins variants, so HD 778 fit is a dimensional check, not a reason to relabel or replace the shocks automatically.

## 6. Wiring, controls and audio integration

Keep the existing **V2 + Maestro and AIO-6 LTE together**. The later owner shop note supersedes the earlier brief that discussed them as alternatives. The goal is improved thumb control of the Soundstream and motorcycle while preserving factory functions; a true rotary controller remains an unverified option, not required purchased equipment.

| Circuit or function | Planned relationship | Bench / on-bike demonstration |
|---|---|---|
| Factory switches and radio | Factory controls → existing harness/Maestro → V2. | Record working scroll/select/back, volume, tracks, voice and phone functions in the actual apps. Verify normal lights, horn, indicators, cruise and start/stop. |
| Main audio | V2 → existing Hertz amplifier → the eight existing Hertz speakers. | Identify amp, channel wiring, loads, polarity and driver condition; set filters/gain to the actual driver system. Do not assign a model from a representative photo. |
| AIO-6 LTE and remote | Retained AIO-6 LTE with its allocated mount/quick release and delivered **CGRC Pro**. | Demonstrate its supported controls separately; do not promise that its remote controls the V2 or motorcycle. Test the owner's desired phone/audio route, reconnection, navigation prompts, calls and microphone selection with both screens present. |
| Radar and camera | SR-1 with its chosen indicators or ordered radar mirrors; XR-1 as a separate camera system. | Establish radar/mirror mounting and M10 adapter needs. Radar mirrors replace the separate indicator function; do not duplicate harness assumptions. Confirm camera view, recording and access to storage. |
| Accessory power | AIO quick release, TR100, Koso USB, heated seat and other allocated loads. | Record each device's feed, switched/constant requirement, fuse, ground and actual connector. The AIO quick release uses vehicle power, not USB power. Check charging operation and key-off behavior after installation. |
| Removable luggage | Labeled bike-side and luggage-side disconnects with a saved pinout. | Separate speaker, lighting and amplifier-power requirements; choose contacts for each actual circuit. Remove and reconnect each bag/Tour-Pak without loose conductors, strain or left/right ambiguity. |
| TPMS | Ordered external sensors on the actual wheels/valves. | Check valve, sensor and caliper clearance; pair correctly and compare readings with a reference gauge. |

Mount and test electronics before final loom wrapping and panel closure. Keep fuses and service connectors accessible. Record the exact connector and pinout rather than treating earlier Amphenol, DEUTSCH, speakON or custom-control suggestions as a purchased harness BOM. The separately purchased VAULT battery has no confirmed allocation to this motorcycle.
