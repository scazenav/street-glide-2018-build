## Documentation still required at the bench

- **PM M8 Vision cam cover:** obtain the exact supplied/manufacturer instruction. The generic PM Cam Cover ZIP contains an older Scallop-cover document and is not a verified substitute.
- **HPi high-flow rail and 62 mm assembly / injectors:** preserve the packed instructions and the actual fuel/tuning data. Where research found only product or support pages, the catalog marks that distinction.
- **Exact Rekluse parts:** owner-confirmed model family is sufficient to retain the system; exact component labels are needed to choose settings and replacement parts.
- **Historical and returned parts:** resolve physical disposition at intake, particularly overlapping bodywork and any return whose completion is not established. Do not mark an item installed solely because it was delivered.

## Close-out format

For each open check, record **date · mechanic · actual part/setting or measurement · instruction used · result · remaining action**. Attach photographs for labels, final wire routing and any nonstandard bracket or adapter arrangement. Update the installed inventory and leave removed/spare parts clearly labeled.
