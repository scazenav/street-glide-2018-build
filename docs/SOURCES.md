## Source precedence

1. Owner's latest explicit corrections: the correct BAKER compensator is ordered; the corrected OG brake set is Touring. The October 20 inventory specifies Touring **hub mount**, dual disc, black calipers / gold spacers / black brackets.
2. Later order confirmations, packing slips, delivery updates, cancellations and seller correspondence establish the latest documented purchase status.
3. **Street_Glide_Master_Parts.xlsx**, October 20, 2026: all 34 inventory rows reconciled in [Prior inventory](PRIOR_INVENTORY.md). Embedded source notes and images were read, not inferred from the filename.
4. **Street_Glide_Controls_Shop_Note.md**, October 15: existing Soundstream V2, Maestro, CHIGEE, and Hertz audio; both screens and the audio stay. The exact LTE variant is supplied by the later inventory.
5. **Street_Glide_Build_Map.html**, October 15, and **Street_Glide_Current_and_Desired_Setup.md**, October 14, provide historical context only where not superseded.
6. Public manufacturer documents and actual seller product pages establish specifications and installation resources. A catalog match is distinguished from an SKU recovered from the owner's order.

## Purchase evidence

Gmail order searches covered the build's known vendors and bike-related purchases, including S&S/Cycle Gear, Fuel Moto, Parts Giant, BAKER, Team Dream Rides/OG, HPi/Hoban, OilBud, Kraus, Koso, Dirty Bird, J&P, Amazon and eBay. Shop supplied October order status and older accessory history, including CHIGEE, mounts, speaker hardware, and historical accessory candidates. Per-part records retain order references and Gmail message IDs for later traceability; raw messages, addresses, payment information, authenticated order-status links, and tracking credentials are omitted.

These searches cannot prove that a cash purchase, deleted email, disconnected account, or unrecorded part does not exist. The October 20 owner inventory closes many gaps in transaction-only history. Physical receiving and installation checkboxes remain blank deliberately.

## Deduplication and units

- The Sand Dune fuel tank (eBay 358446083676 / Cyclewarehouse 83741) is one purchase even though Gmail and Shop both show it.
- The Sand Dune paint (eBay 358724143949 / Cyclewarehouse 84959) is likewise one purchase.
- Components listed inside a parent kit do not create additional purchased units.
- The broad Sand Dune CVO-style bodywork description is retained as context, not another kit stacked on top of the itemized body orders.
- The existing Rekluse clutch, basket and slave are one assembly history; the slave and six spare springs also have individual index records for service reference.
- One pair, pack, assembly, or kit is not necessarily one individual piece. Where an email excerpt did not establish quantity, the field is explicitly unconfirmed.

## Photos, manuals and applicability

Photos come from manufacturer/seller pages or the earlier owner inventory. The head photograph is identified as an owner-provided photo. Other photos generally show the product or product family, not the condition of a received unit. A family image does not establish an unknown SKU. The outdated Dirty Bird representative photo from the workbook is not used for DBC359.

Exact installation sheets are linked where found. Some products only have official HTML instructions, an installation video, a service-parts drawing, a support page, or a manufacturer instruction finder. These are labeled by type. A reference link does not mean an exact installation manual was located. See [documentation gaps](DOCUMENTATION_GAPS.md) and [downloaded manuals](../manuals/README.md).

Internal parts sometimes have no standalone retail page. Such rows link the manufacturer's illustrated kit/parts reference and preserve known service numbers. No guessed purchase URLs or torque values are supplied. Consult the applicable Harley service manual and exact component instructions for actual workshop procedures.

Product photographs, logos and manufacturer manuals remain the property of their respective owners. This private build handoff includes source attribution and should not be republished as a commercial catalog.
