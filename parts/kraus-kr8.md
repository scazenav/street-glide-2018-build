## Build notes

- 30mm stem; standard length performance valving; black components/black forks; 2018 Street Glide.
- Separate quote items EL-2000 brake lines, 22-1068 neck bearings and titanium brake hardware must not be counted again as included KR8 components.
- Manufacturer specifies 108mm radial calipers; retain OG Touring kit but use KR8's intended mounts and verify rotor centering during assembly.
- Factory fairing/nacelle may require template-guided modifications.
- Standard-length PERFORMANCE valving is the quote selection; the website's 21-inch wheel restriction explicitly refers to standard-length TOURING valving and should not be indiscriminately applied.
